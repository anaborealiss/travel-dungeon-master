# Split Travel Notes

## General vibe
Split is a coastal Croatian city with Roman history, seaside walks, stone streets, beaches, island trips, cafés, many street cats, and a relaxed Mediterranean atmosphere.

## Good for cozy travel
- Diocletian's Palace: historic stone streets, small squares, cafés, and atmospheric corners.
- Riva Promenade: seaside walking area with cafés and views of the harbor.
- Marjan Hill: green area with walking paths, viewpoints, and quieter nature.
- Bačvice Beach: popular beach close to the center, good for a relaxed break.
- Varoš: old neighborhood with narrow streets and local atmosphere.
- Cat encounters: Split has many friendly-looking street cats around old streets, squares, and quiet corners; they can become small cozy side quests during walks.

## Crowded places
- Diocletian's Palace can be crowded during the middle of the day.
- Riva Promenade is popular, especially in the evening.
- Bačvice Beach can be busy in summer.
- Ferry areas can be crowded when people travel to nearby islands.

## Good quest ideas
- Main quest: Explore Diocletian's Palace and its hidden stone passages.
- Side quest: Walk up Marjan Hill for sea views.
- Cozy side quest: Spot the local cats while wandering through stone streets.
- Treasure: Watch the sunset from the Riva Promenade or Marjan viewpoint.
- Sea quest: Take a short island trip if the traveler has extra time.
- Tavern stop: Rest in a seaside café.
- Food buff: Try black risotto for a mysterious sea-power bonus.
- Sweet buff: Try fritule for a small morale boost.
- Familiar encounter: Meet a Split street cat, but respect its space and do not disturb it.

## Food and buff items
- Black risotto: a traditional seafood dish made with cuttlefish or squid ink; works as a dramatic sea-power buff.
- Peka: meat or seafood cooked slowly under a bell-like lid; works as a strong stamina buff.
- Fritule: small Croatian sweet fried dough balls; good as a morale buff.
- Fresh seafood: useful as a coastal energy buff after walking near the sea.
- Soparnik: a traditional Dalmatian savory pie with chard; good as a local heritage buff.

## Slow travel tips
- Spend time wandering inside Diocletian's Palace instead of rushing through it.
- Add a long café break on the Riva Promenade.
- Visit Marjan Hill when the traveler wants a quieter nature break.
- Keep beach time flexible instead of scheduling every hour.
- Add time for spontaneous cat encounters in the old streets.
- Avoid planning too many island trips in a short visit.

## Fast-paced travel tips
- If the traveler likes packed schedules, combine Diocletian's Palace, Riva Promenade, Varoš, Marjan Hill, and Bačvice Beach in one day.
- Start early if the traveler wants both city exploration and beach time.
- Use ferry trips only if there is enough time and energy.
- Keep food stops close to the route to avoid losing time.
- Add cat spotting as a light optional side quest, not a fixed schedule item.
- Warn the traveler if the plan includes too much walking in heat.
